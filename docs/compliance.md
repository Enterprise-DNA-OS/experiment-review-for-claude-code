# Record checks and their sources

These checks remind a trusted operator to review records. They do not decide whether a business complies with the law, enforce a retention schedule, verify consent, certify an experiment or inspect hosting security. Experiment reports can be aggregate-only; owner names, notes and extra imported columns can still contain personal information.

| Rule | Check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NZ-IPP1 | Experiment has a recorded purpose for retained review material | [NZ Privacy Commissioner, Principle 1](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/1/) |
| NZ-IPP9 | Retention review date is present and not due | [NZ Privacy Commissioner, Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) |
| NZ-IPP5 | Access review date is present and not due | [NZ Privacy Commissioner, Principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) |

Sources checked 7 November 2026. The legal principles concern necessary collection, appropriate security and not retaining personal information longer than needed. The review dates are this tool's operating reminders, not statutory time limits. A passing record check is not proof that the review occurred or the infrastructure is secure.

Before importing, inspect the columns and remove unnecessary personal information in a separately retained working copy. The importer keeps source columns for provenance. Use the operator's approved purpose and review dates with privacy-review. Keep exports and reports private, configure access and backups, and verify actual controls outside this application. Australian deployments need a review against the rules applicable to that business.
